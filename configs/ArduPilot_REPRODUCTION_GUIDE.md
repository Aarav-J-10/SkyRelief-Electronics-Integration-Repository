# ArduPilot REPRODUCTION GUIDE

The historical workflow names Pixhawk 2.4.8, ArduPilot and Mission Planner. Identify exact board first. A newly selected supported firmware/target needs its own revision record, controller orientation, geometry, motor order, receiver protocol, monitor calibration, sensors and failsafes. Nothing in the estimate script supplies historical values. Do not connect propulsion or write parameters from this package. Consult https://ardupilot.org/copter/ and official powering/gripper guidance.
